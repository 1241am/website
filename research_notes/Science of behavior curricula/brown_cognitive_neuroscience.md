## 10. Hypothesis test: Behavior -> experiment -> measurement -> cognition -> neural mechanism -> computational model

### Verdict: MODIFY (partially supported)
- Supported: bulletin says goal is "understanding cognition from a neural perspective" via both behavioral/experimental and computational directions; methods (0900/1900), fMRI (1490), and computational courses (1291, 1492 "bridge the gap between biology and behavior") all exist — sources above.
- Modification: the structure is not a single linear chain but two parallel directions (behavioral/experimental and computational/modeling) that converge on the neural perspective; models (1492) also generate predictions in the reverse direction ("neural mechanisms inform the mind and cognitive/computational constraints inform mechanisms" — course blurb, [source](https://ski.clps.brown.edu/cogsim.html)), i.e., bidirectional, and students may choose a track emphasizing only one end. Whether every student meets all six stages is not guaranteed by requirements (electives are free within themes).
