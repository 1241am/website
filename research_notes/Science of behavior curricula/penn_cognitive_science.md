## 1. Degree requirements

### Takeaway
The BA is 16 course units: one core course (COGS 1001), six breadth units, nine concentration units. Concentrations named in catalog URLs: Computation and Cognition, Cognitive Neuroscience, Language & Mind, Individualized. Honors requires a senior thesis.

### Cited Findings
- BA total is 16 unique units: 1 core (COGS 1001), 6 breadth, 9 concentration; breadth courses do not count again toward the concentration. VERIFIED (via summary) — [Penn COGS FAQ/major page](https://web.sas.upenn.edu/cogsci/?p=630); [Major page](https://web.sas.upenn.edu/cogsci/major/)
- Core: Introduction to Cognitive Science (COGS 1001 / CIS 1400 / LING 1005 / PHIL 1840 / PSYC 1333), offered Fall only. VERIFIED — [web.sas.upenn.edu/cogsci/?p=630](https://web.sas.upenn.edu/cogsci/?p=630)
- Breadth: one course from each of six areas, including Psychology, Computation, Language, Philosophy (full list of six not confirmed by me). VERIFIED partially — [same](https://web.sas.upenn.edu/cogsci/?p=630)
- Four concentration BA program pages exist in the catalog: Computation and Cognition, Cognitive Neuroscience, Language & Mind, Individualized. VERIFIED — [Comp & Cog](https://catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/cognitive-science-computation-cognition-ba/), [Cog Neuro](https://catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/cognitive-science-cognitive-neuroscience-ba/), [Language & Mind](https://catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/cognitive-science-language-mind-ba/), [Individualized](https://catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/cognitive-science-individualized-ba/)
- Comp & Cog summary: breadth items include PSYC 1210 (Intro to Brain and Behavior), a math course (STAT 1110, MATH 1400 or MATH 1410), and the intro COGS course; Computation: one of CIS 1100, CIS 1200, CIS 1210; Language: LING 0001; Philosophy: one of PHIL 1170, 1800, 1810, 1830, 2620, 2640, 2660, 2680, 3200, 3620, 4600; plus 9 units concentration electives; 36 units total for the degree. VERIFIED (summary; exact grouping of items between breadth and concentration unclear) — [Comp & Cog catalog](https://catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/cognitive-science-computation-cognition-ba/)
- Cog Neuro: required Introduction to Brain and Behavior (NRSC 1110); a statistics course (STAT 1110 or higher) strongly recommended. Breadth options named: Language (LING 0001; LING 0500 accepted outside Comp & Cog), Philosophy (PHIL 1170, 1710, 2260, 2640), Neuroscience (NRSC 2249/PSYC 1230, NRSC 1110/BIOL 1110/PSYC 1210), Mathematics (STAT 1110, MATH 1400, MATH 1410/1510). VERIFIED (summary) — [Cog Neuro catalog](https://catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/cognitive-science-cognitive-neuroscience-ba/); [Major page](https://web.sas.upenn.edu/cogsci/major/)
- Language & Mind: 36 units minimum for graduation; minimum GPA 3.0 overall and 3.5 in major courses (as stated in the summary). VERIFIED (summary) — [Language & Mind catalog](https://catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/cognitive-science-language-mind-ba/)
- Honors: 3.5 GPA in major courses, 3.0 cumulative, plus senior-year research project approved by the Program Director/Associate Director and supervised by the concentration advisor; COGS 3998 (Senior Thesis), ~30-40 pages; COGS 3999 is Independent Study. VERIFIED (summary; page is under an older "major_old" URL, so may be dated) — [Independent Study/Honors Thesis Guide](https://web.sas.upenn.edu/cogsci/program/major_old/independent-study-honor-thesis-research-guide/)
- Catalog attribute pages list approved courses per concentration: ACGC (Computation and Cognition), ACGN (Cognitive Neuroscience), ACGL (Language & Mind). Full lists not read. VERIFIED existence — [ACGC](https://catalog.upenn.edu/attributes/acgc/), [ACGN](https://catalog.upenn.edu/attributes/acgn/), [ACGL](https://catalog.upenn.edu/attributes/acgl/)
- A Cognitive Science minor exists. VERIFIED — [Minor](https://catalog.upenn.edu/undergraduate/programs/cognitive-science-minor/)
- The 2026-27 University Catalog exists, so the current catalog year is 2026-27. VERIFIED — [Catalog home](https://catalog.upenn.edu/)

## 10. Hypothesis test (Perception -> representation -> learning -> memory -> language -> reasoning -> decision -> computation)
### Takeaway
MODIFY (weakly supported, low confidence). Evidence supports the topic list (perception, learning, memory, decision making, language) in COGS 1001's catalog description and computation as modeling tool, but the curriculum is organized as discipline-based tracks (computation, language, neuroscience, philosophy, individualized) rather than a linear pipeline, and computation appears as a parallel method/track, not the final stage.
