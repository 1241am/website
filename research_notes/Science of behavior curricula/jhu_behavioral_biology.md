## 1. Degree requirements (credits, tracks, capstone, advising sheets)

### Takeaway
Requirements page (search-summarized) shows a math/science foundation, a 5-course-type core, 5 tagged electives (3 Biobehavioral, 2 Social Science), one semester of research/internship/Utila, a 0.5-credit Connections seminar, and a senior seminar. No tracks were seen. Total credit count not obtained.

### Cited Findings
- Foundations: Calculus I & II; Chemistry I & II with lab; two biology options (General Biology, Genetics, Biochemistry, Cell Biology, or Comparative Physiology); Statistics. Physics I & II with lab is NOT required for students graduating 2027 and later. SEARCH-SEEN — [Krieger requirements](https://krieger.jhu.edu/behavioralbiology/the-major/requirements/)
- Core: Foundations of Brain, Behavior, and Cognition; Human Origins; Animal Behavior; Neuroscience Lab; Behavioral Evolution Lab or Animal Behavior and Communication Lab (labs writing-intensive for 2027+ graduates); Senior seminar. SEARCH-SEEN — [Krieger requirements](https://krieger.jhu.edu/behavioralbiology/academics/requirements/)
- Electives: three with "Biobehavioral" tag (BEHB-BIOBEH) and two with "Socsci" tag (BEHB-SOCSCI). Tags changed across years; check the current year. SEARCH-SEEN — [Krieger courses page](https://krieger.jhu.edu/behavioralbiology/the-major/courses/)
- One semester of Research, Internship, or Intersession study-abroad trip to Utila Island; one semester of Connections in Behavioral Biology (0.5 credit; AS.290.500). SEARCH-SEEN — [requirements](https://krieger.jhu.edu/behavioralbiology/the-major/requirements/); [Semester.ly AS.290.500](https://jhu.semester.ly/c/AS.290.500)
- At least 15 credits at 300 level or above; two writing-intensive courses for 2027+ graduates; C- or better required in major courses; no satisfactory/unsatisfactory. SEARCH-SEEN — [requirements](https://krieger.jhu.edu/behavioralbiology/the-major/requirements/)
- Program is an interdepartmental, interdivisional area major studying natural and social sciences in relation to human and animal behavior. SEARCH-SEEN — [people page](https://krieger.jhu.edu/behavioralbiology/people/)
- Catalogue pages exist: [degree page](https://e-catalogue.jhu.edu/arts-sciences/full-time-residential-programs/degree-programs/behavioral-biology/behavioral-biology-bachelor-arts/); [AS.290 course list](https://e-catalogue.jhu.edu/course-descriptions/behavioral_biology/); earlier-year requirements at [past courses](https://krieger.jhu.edu/behavioralbiology/the-major/courses/past-courses) (contents not read).
